Event Brief

DOC: PC-CRT-BRIEF.V4

The Circuit de Verona Criterium Classic is our premiere high-velocity street-style test held across the perimeter access ring of Buddh International. Riders will contest a closed 2.6 km perimeter loop consisting of 4 distinct technical chicanes, 2 high-speed straightaways, and a tactical finish drag.

01 / Paceline Directive

Strict double-echelon rotation in neutral laps. Hold predictable lines when sprinting or taking tactical feeds.

02 / Lap Card Protocol

Countdown cards display at start/finish gantry from Lap 5. Bell lap signaled audibly and via electronic display.

03 / Free Lap Rule

Recognized mechanical mishaps permit a 1-lap pit return until 8 km remaining. Commissaire approval required.

Safety Protocol & Requirements

ZERO TOLERANCE POLICY

Mandatory Helmet Standard

CE-EN 1078 or CPSC certified hard-shell cycling helmet worn and buckled at all times within circuit perimeter.

Drop Handlebar Requirement

Traditional road drop bars only. Clip-on aero bars, bullhorns, barend levers, and disc wheels are strictly prohibited.

emergency
Functional Dual Braking

Both front and rear calipers/rotors must be operative with minimum 60% pad life. Fixed gear bikes are disallowed.

Dedicated ALS paramedic units stationed at Turns 2 and 4. Mobile sag-wagon sweep follow behind the main field.
